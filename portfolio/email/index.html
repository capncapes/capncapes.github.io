Brandon's email development journey

Brandon Caples has always had a preference for working with “vanilla” HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which led him further into email development. He had been putting together email campaigns for years by the time he started coding emails, but his development skills allowed him to craft better, more performant emails.

He found his passion for the unique challenges of email development. There are many quirks, hacks, and bugs when it comes to HTML emails rendering consistently across email clients like Outlook, Gmail, and Yahoo, but Brandon loves the challenge.

Throughout his work, he's always prioritized responsiveness, accessibility, and deliverability, ensuring that every email he creates not only looks great but also performs well in the inbox.
